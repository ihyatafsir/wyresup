The Lifespan as an Irreplaceable Blessing

Shaykh Muhammad al-Hasan al-Dedew (الشيخ محمد الحسن ولد الددو) • Commentary on Surah al-Munafiqun (9–11)

English Translation & Quranic References

فالإنسان قامت عليه الحجة بهذا العمر الشخصي وهو نعمة عظيمة لا يستشعرها الإنسان إلا عندما يموت...
"Indeed, decisive proof has been established against man through this individual lifespan—which is an immense blessing that a person never truly appreciates until he dies. The dwellers of the graves wish to return to this worldly life, even for a fleeting moment..."
﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ...﴾
«O you who have believed, let not your wealth nor your children distract you from the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that—then those are the true losers. And spend [in charity] from what We have provided you before death comes to one of you, and he pleads: 'My Lord, if only You would delay me for a brief term, so I would give charity and be among the righteous!' But never will Allah delay a soul when its appointed time has come.» [Surah al-Munafiqun: 9–11]
